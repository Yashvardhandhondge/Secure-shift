Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the SecureShift prototype.

What is Cloud Governance?

Cloud Governance compares the Azure permissions a user or service has been granted with the permissions they actually used, gives an Over-Privilege Score from 0 to 100 and builds a smaller custom role.

What is EMDT?

EMDT (Enterprise Modernization Digital Twin) models applications, APIs, databases and their links, so teams can simulate a modernization before spending money.

Is any real data used?

No. Sprint 1 is a front-end prototype. All data is hardcoded sample data.
