How the category is decided
  • Each parameter gets a score from 0 to 100.
  • EMSI = sum of (score × weight). Weights add up to 100%.
  • Foundation 0-25 · Growth 25-45 · Enterprise 45-70 · Strategic 70-100
  • The category sets the subscription price and the effort multiplier used in simulations.
